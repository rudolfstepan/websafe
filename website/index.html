Frequently asked questions

Clarity is part
of protection.

Still have questions? Use GitHub issues to ask questions or report false positives.

Does WebSafe replace antivirus software?+

No. WebSafe prevents common browser threats but cannot perform a full file analysis. Keep your browser's built-in Safe Browsing protection and up-to-date antivirus software enabled.

Are ordinary downloads blocked?+

Ordinary PDFs, images, videos and archives are not blocked solely because of their extension. WebSafe focuses on executable, script-capable or disguised files.

Does WebSafe work in incognito mode?+

Yes, after you enable “Allow in incognito” in your browser's extension details.

Can I bypass false positives?+

You can add trusted domains as local exceptions. Use exceptions deliberately and sparingly.

Is WebSafe really open source?+

Yes. The entire project is available under the MIT License, including the build script, tests and security documentation.
