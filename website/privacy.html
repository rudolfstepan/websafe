Transparent and local

Privacy without the fine print.

WebSafe processes protection signals locally in your browser. There is no telemetry, no user account and no data sent to the developer.

← Back to WebSafe

Overview

WebSafe does not send visited URLs, page content or download information to the developer or an external analytics service. The extension works within the local browser profile in Chrome, Brave and Chromium-based browsers.

Data processed locally

  • The URL and hostname of a visited page for local filtering decisions
  • Page titles, metadata and limited excerpts of visible text
  • Links and media addresses to identify suspicious embedded content
  • A download's filename, source, MIME type and browser-reported danger status
  • Settings, exception lists and aggregate blocking counters

Storage

Settings, domain exceptions and counters are stored using chrome.storage.local in the local browser profile. WebSafe operates no server and has no central user database.

This website stores your manually selected language locally in your browser. Without a saved choice, it uses the browser language; languages other than German fall back to English.

Data transfers

WebSafe itself does not transmit protection data. External links on this website lead to the Chrome Web Store (Google) and GitHub, where the respective providers' privacy policies apply. Your browser's built-in Safe Browsing protection operates independently of WebSafe according to your browser settings.

Permissions

The extension needs page access to detect pop-ups, navigation and overlays, plus download permission to cancel risky downloads in time. Permissions are used exclusively for the documented protection features.

Deleting data

When you remove the extension, the browser deletes its storage. Reinstalling the extension can also reset its settings.

Open Source

The complete implementation, all filter rules and automated tests are available in the public GitHub repository.
