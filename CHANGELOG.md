## [1.1.0] - 2026-09-11

### Added

- Local multi-layer filtering for explicit sites, embedded content and overlays.
- Strict blocking of automatic and cross-origin scripted pop-ups.
- SafeSearch enforcement for common search engines.
- Scareware and fake virus-warning detection.
- Risky download protection for executable, script, macro and disguised files.
- Blocking of public downloads transported over plain HTTP.
- Harmless local manual test page and automated filter/manifest tests.
- Options, domain allowlist, block counters and dedicated warning pages.
